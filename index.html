About the Curriculum

This curriculum invites students to learn AI by building and playing games.

  • Design AI as a game character.
  • Learn how AI learns and the role humans play.
  • Learn how to collaborate with AI.
  • Become a young game designer.

STANDARD

  • Explore the types and forms of data that can be used in AI.
  • Experience the process by which AI is created, and explore the effects of AI on society.

CURRICULUM MAP

No. Topic Key Concept Game Description
1 Designing Your AI Character Human-in-the-loop Students design their own AI sprite and build a scene that shows the role humans play in guiding it.
2 Learning from Data More data improves AI Students feed data tokens to their AI sprite and watch how more data changes what it can do.
3 Learning from Data Data quality matters Students sort good and bad data cards and build a game where data quality decides the outcome.
4 How Generative AI works Patterns → Prediction Students build a word-guessing game, where the AI predicts answers based on patterns it finds.
5-6 Co-intelligence Human + AI collaboration Students design a two-player scene where a human and an AI character team up to solve a task together.

Result

1. AI literacy changes

Significant Gains in Affective & Cognitive Dimensions of AI literacy

Affective and Cognitive dimensions increased significantly; Ethical showed no significant change, though interviews revealed deeper understanding of AI's limits.

2. Relationships

Dimensions of AI literacy Became More Differentiated

Affective-Ethical correlation dropped from significant to near-zero (ρ = .487 → -.144); Affective-Cognitive weakened (ρ = .715 → .406) - suggesting enjoyment, understanding, and ethical judgment followed increasingly independent paths.
